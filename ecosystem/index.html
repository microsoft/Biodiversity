    Project catalogue¶

    The Microsoft AI for Good Lab keeps each biodiversity project in its own repository. This page helps you pick a starting point, shows how the projects connect, and links the model zoo. The home page has the short list of repositories.

    Which project should I use?¶

    How the projects fit together¶

    1. Capture. A SPARROW device, a SPARROWmini node or a commercial camera trap records images or audio in the field.
    2. Receive. Sparrow Comm collects that data over the device API, e-mail, the Reconyx cloud or FTPS.
    3. Analyse. Sparrow Engine runs models from the Sparrow Model Zoo on it: MegaDetector to find animals, then a classifier for species, or acoustic models for sound.
    4. Explore. SPARROW Playground shows each step in a notebook you can copy into your own code.

    Model development happens in the model repositories: MegaDetector, MegaDetector-Classifier, MegaDetector-Acoustic and MegaDetector-Overhead. SPARROW Model Zoo Uploader converts a trained model, checks its outputs against the original, and submits it to the zoo that Sparrow Engine runs.

    Model zoo¶

    The Sparrow Model Zoo holds the lab's models and third-party models for camera traps, acoustics, overhead and marine imagery, and general use. Each model keeps its own licence, so check it before use, especially for commercial work.

    • Catalogue: the Sparrow Engine model zoo catalogue lists every model with its source, licence and citation. Sections: camera-trap detectors, camera-trap classifiers, acoustic detectors, acoustic classifiers, overhead detectors, marine imagery detectors, licensing at a glance.
    • Model files: the Sparrow Model Zoo on Zenodo. This DOI always opens the latest version.
    • MegaDetector accuracy and model sizes: the MegaDetector model zoo page.
    • Earlier weights: the AI4G-Opossum classifier from PyTorch-Wildlife is not in the Sparrow Model Zoo. Its original weights (OpossumClassification_v0.0.0.ckpt) stay on Zenodo record 10023414.

    The model zoo pages that used to live on this site (model_zoo/megadetector/, model_zoo/classifiers/, model_zoo/other_detectors/, model_zoo/bioacoustics/) now redirect to the matching sections of the catalogue.
